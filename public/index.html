Features

Everything you need to control who walks through your door.

AI Risk Engine

Each request is scored on context (time, location), behaviour (anomaly detection) and history (failed attempts) — then granted, stepped up, or denied with an alert.

Permanent & Guest Access

Trusted members get their own PIN. Guests receive time-limited PINs over WhatsApp — valid for minutes, days, or a single visit.

Entry Notifications

The admin is notified every time a permanent user unlocks, and every PIN event is logged with IP and location.

Event Log & Analytics

Full access-event database, decision distribution charts, and a simulation lab to reproduce the research results.

ESP32 Ready

Works in simulation today; flash the provided firmware later and the same website drives your physical lock.

Google Sign-In

Admins and users sign in with Google. One lock per admin, unlimited users per lock.

How It Works

From box to first unlock in three steps.

1. Get your lock

Order a Pismartlok above or connect your own ESP32 with our open firmware.

2. Register it

Sign in with Google, open the admin console, and register your lock's device ID. Then add users with their phone numbers.

3. Unlock smartly

Users sign in at the user portal, verify with OTP if needed, enter their PIN — the AI decides in milliseconds.
