Privacy Policy

Home

1. Data We Collect

Google profile (name, email, picture) for sign-in; user IDs, names and phone numbers you register; access events including timestamps, IP-derived location, and risk scores.

2. How We Use It

To authenticate you, deliver OTPs and PINs, run the adaptive risk engine, show admins their lock's event log, and improve the service.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Geolocation lookups use third-party IP services; WhatsApp-style notifications are simulated in the pilot and stay on our servers.

4. Retention

Access events are kept while your lock is active. Ask your admin to delete your user record to stop further collection.

5. Security

Sessions expire after 24 hours and PINs are stored as hashes. No system is perfect — report suspected misuse to support@pismartlok.example.
